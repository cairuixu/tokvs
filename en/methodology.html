Our Methodology

How we compare exchanges, what we check, and where our money comes from.

Updated Oct 2026 · By TokVS

How we score exchanges

We score exchanges on five dimensions. The weights below are our editorial framework — they describe how we judge, not a laboratory measurement. Regulation and availability 30%, fees 25%, safety 20%, beginner experience 15%, coins and features 10%. Availability is weighted first on purpose: the cheapest exchange you cannot legally use is worthless to you.

Each dimension is judged from primary sources where possible: the exchange's official fee page, the regulator's register, the exchange's proof-of-reserves page, and its published security features. Where we rely on a secondary source, we say so on the page.

What we check, and when

Fees: base spot and futures maker/taker rates at the standard (VIP0) tier, plus withdrawal network fees. Availability: which countries and states each exchange serves, from its official terms and restricted-countries pages. Safety: proof of reserves, emergency funds, account security features, and the enforcement record — including the negative parts.

Every comparison page shows the month its figures were compiled, and availability tables show an exact check date. Fees and availability change often; a page that does not show its check date should not be trusted, including ours if the date is stale.

What we never do

No exchange can pay for a higher rank, a better score, or the removal of a negative finding. Commission does not affect rankings or scores. If an exchange's availability or fees get worse, its position on our pages gets worse, regardless of what it pays us.

We do not publish a figure we cannot trace to a source. Where a figure is approximate — for example network fees that vary with congestion — we label it approximate and tell you where the exact figure appears (usually the withdrawal screen).

How this site makes money

Through affiliate links to Binance, OKX and Bybit. If you sign up through those links, the exchange pays us a share of the fees you generate; it does not cost you extra. Our Bitget button is a plain link to the official site — we currently have no rebate arrangement with Bitget. See the full affiliate disclosure.

FAQ

Do exchanges pay you to rank higher?

No. No exchange can pay for a higher rank, a better score, or the removal of a negative finding. Commission does not affect our rankings or scores.

How often do you recheck fees and availability?

Comparison pages show the month their figures were compiled, and availability tables show an exact check date. We recheck when we update a page, and we show the date so you can judge freshness yourself.

Why do your picks differ from other sites?

Because the weights differ. We weight availability first: an exchange you cannot legally use scores badly with us no matter how cheap it is. Other sites weight fees or coin counts first, so their order differs legitimately.
