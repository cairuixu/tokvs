OKX review: fees, rebates, deposits & withdrawals explained

Compiled October 2026 · Independent review, not affiliated with OKX

At a glance

OKX is one of the most complete exchanges out there, with slightly lower spot maker fees than Binance, a built-in Web3 wallet, and solid multilingual support — a good fit if you want trading plus on-chain activity in one place.

Deposits & withdrawals

  • Deposits: express P2P buys, third-party payments, and on-chain deposits all supported.
  • Withdrawals: sell via P2P or withdraw to your wallet; the Web3 wallet enables direct on-chain interaction.
  • Highlight: seamless switching between the exchange account and Web3 wallet — DeFi-friendly.

Pros & cons

Pros

  • 0.08% spot maker fee — the lowest of the four
  • Built-in Web3 wallet for easy on-chain activity
  • Good multilingual support and community

Cons

  • Futures depth slightly behind Binance overall
  • Slower small-cap listings than the top players
  • Feature-rich, so beginners need time to get oriented

Who it's for

Traders who use both centralized trading and DeFi, and low-frequency traders who value cheap spot maker fees. For high-frequency futures, costs are roughly on par with Binance.
