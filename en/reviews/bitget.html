Bitget review: fees, rebates, deposits & withdrawals explained

Compiled October 2026 · Independent review, not affiliated with Bitget

At a glance

Bitget is known for copy trading and ranks among the most beginner-friendly exchanges. Its 0.06% futures taker fee is the highest of the four — best for low-frequency traders and copy-trading users; high-frequency futures traders should compare before choosing.

Deposits & withdrawals

  • Deposits: express buy, P2P, and on-chain deposits, with clear beginner guidance.
  • Withdrawals: sell via P2P or withdraw to your own wallet.
  • Highlight: copy trading lets you mirror top traders' strategies — beginner-friendly, but copy trading doesn't guarantee profits.

Pros & cons

Pros

  • Mature copy-trading features, great beginner onboarding
  • Up to 50% rebates — competitive
  • Clean app, quick to learn

Cons

  • 0.06% futures taker fee — highest of the four
  • Mid-range trading depth overall
  • Shorter track record than the other three

Who it's for

Beginners who want to learn via copy trading. One reminder: copy trading isn't a guaranteed win — the traders you follow can lose too, so only risk what you can afford.
