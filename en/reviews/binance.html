Binance review: fees, rebates, deposits & withdrawals explained

Compiled October 2026 · Independent review, not affiliated with Binance

At a glance

Binance is the world's largest crypto exchange by trading volume, with the widest coin selection and deepest liquidity — ideal if you care about depth and variety. KYC is strict, though, and it's unavailable in some countries and regions.

Rebate program

Sign up through a rebate link for up to 50% back on spot and up to 30% on futures — exact rates and validity follow Binance's official rebate policy. Rebates are subsidized by the exchange; your fee rates stay the same.

Deposits & withdrawals

  • Deposits: P2P trading (buy crypto directly with fiat), third-party payment channels, on-chain deposits.
  • Withdrawals: sell via P2P or withdraw to your own wallet; large withdrawals may trigger a risk review.
  • Note: restricted in some countries and regions — confirm availability in yours before signing up.

Pros & cons

Pros

  • Deepest liquidity worldwide, minimal slippage on large orders
  • Widest coin selection, fast new listings
  • Mature product suite: spot, futures, earn products, Launchpool — everything in one place

Cons

  • Strict KYC with a relatively tedious process
  • Unavailable in some countries and regions
  • Huge user base means slower customer support

Who it's for

Traders who want deep order books, access to small-cap coins, or one account for spot + futures + earn products. If you only trade major-coin spot, the differences are minor; high-frequency futures traders get the best deal here.
