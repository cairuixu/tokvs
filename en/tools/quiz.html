FAQ

Is this quiz advice on where to put my money?

No. It matches your answers to three mainstream exchanges using public fee schedules and product facts. It is not investment advice, and availability varies by country — always check whether an exchange serves your region before signing up.

Why only Binance, OKX and Bybit?

They are the three large global exchanges this site compares in depth, with published fee schedules we can verify. If you are in the US, note that Binance.com does not serve US customers — US beginners usually start with Coinbase or Kraken instead.

Do the fees in the result change?

Yes — exchanges adjust fee schedules, and VIP tiers change effective rates. Treat the numbers here as a snapshot checked in October 2026 and confirm the current schedule on the exchange's official fee page before trading.
