Universal sign-up guide: claim your exchange rebate

Compiled October 2026 · Applies to Binance / OKX / Bybit / Bitget

This site contains affiliate links. Registering through our links won't cost you extra.

Why sign up with a rebate link

A rebate is the exchange's official way of sharing fee revenue with referrers: sign up through a rebate link and a slice of every trade's fee comes back to you (or your referrer shares part of theirs with you). Your fee rates don't change — the rebate is extra, paid by the exchange. Registering directly on the official site means leaving that subsidy on the table.

Lock in your rebate in 7 steps

  1. Open the official site via the rebate link: confirm the page shows a rebate badge (e.g. "rebate XX%") before registering. Trust only the official domain — watch out for phishing lookalikes.
  2. Create your account: sign up with email or phone and set a strong password.
  3. Complete KYC: upload your ID and do the face check — required for deposits, withdrawals, and full features.
  4. Set up security: enable Google Authenticator (2FA), set a fund password, turn on the anti-phishing code.
  5. Confirm the rebate is attached: check the "Rebate center / My referrals" page for your rebate rate. If it's missing, the link didn't carry over — re-registering with the link is the only fix (you can't add it after the fact).
  6. Fund your account: beginners should start with express P2P buys — try a small amount first, then scale up once you're comfortable.
  7. Start trading: practice with small spot trades first — get comfortable with orders, take-profits and stop-losses before touching futures.

4 traps to avoid

  • Trying to add a rebate after registering: impossible — you'd have to open a new account. Get the link right in step one.
  • Farming rebates with duplicate accounts: explicitly banned everywhere — get caught and your account is closed with rebates wiped.
  • Phishing sites: trust only official domains — never click "sign-up links" from chat groups or texts.
  • Rebate promises: "up to" is a ceiling, not a guarantee — actual terms follow the official rebate policy.
